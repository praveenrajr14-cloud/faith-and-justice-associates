Our Practice & Philosophy எங்கள் நோக்கம் & நெறிமுறைகள்

Faith & Justice Associates and Consultants was established with a singular objective: to provide property buyers, sellers, families, and corporations with transparent, rigorous, and dependable legal guidance in real estate matters.

Property transactions represent major lifetime milestones and substantial capital commitments. Even a minor oversight in parent title tracing, encumbrance records, revenue classification, or statutory approvals can lead to decades of litigation.

Under the leadership of Lead Consultant Pushpa T (Chennai) along with dedicated associate advocates in Chennai and Nagercoil, our firm has built a reputation for thorough scrutiny, unambiguous legal opinions, and precision deed drafting.
