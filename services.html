Conveyance & Transfer Deeds

பரிவர்த்தனை பத்திரங்கள்

Sale & Gift Conveyancing
  • Sale Deed (கிரய பத்திரம்): Complete formulation of absolute sale deed with clear boundaries, market value consideration, indemnity against future claims, and possession delivery clauses.
  • Gift Deed (இஷ்டதான / தான பத்திரம்): Voluntary transfer of immovable property without monetary consideration to loved ones, drafted with irrevocable statutory covenants.
  • Agreement of Sale (விற்பனை உடன்படிக்கை): Comprehensive agreement outlining milestone payments, time frames, and default remedies.
⚖️

Family & Succession Deeds

குடும்ப & பாகப்பிரிவினை பத்திரங்கள்

Partition, Release & Settlement
  • Partition Deed (பாகபிரிவினை பத்திரம்): Legally binding division of coparcenary or ancestral joint property among co-owners and legal heirs with exact schedule demarcations.
  • Release Deed (விடுதலை பத்திரம்): Relinquishment of rights, title, and interest by a co-heir or family member in favor of other co-sharers.
  • Settlement Deed (செட்டில்மென்ட் பத்திரம்): Property settlement deeds for family members, often retaining life-estate interest or conditional clauses.
  • Will & Testament (உயில்): Clear, legally ironclad testamentary disposition to avoid probate friction and family disputes.
